ContinuousStrategy

  • Engine: the strategy class handles entry only. All leg handling is in LegManager.
  • Entry (T1): PCR direction, re-checked every 5 minutes, opens a root leg. Window 09:30–15:00. Optional momentum veto (momentumEnabled, order-book imbalance). If cfg.right is set, PCR must agree.
  • Adverse moves: each level at 1x to Nx of slDistance (up to maxLevels) spawns a new opposite-direction leg. Separate hard square-off at squareOffDistance. Averaging into the same leg is possible (averageQuantityMode, postAverageTargetDistance).
  • Target hit: the leg refills at its original entry price. Refills are gated: a nested leg refills only while its parent is alive; child refills are cancelled when a leg stops refilling.
  • Limits: maxProfit is a % of maxInvestment; also a max-loss trip. Supports cooldownSeconds, minPremium, spawn/average quantity modes.
  • Hedging: none. The tree is built from stacked adverse levels.

SimpleContinuousStrategy

  • Entry: one root per day (PCR direction, initialQuantity at market). With onePositionPerSide: true the root buys as many lots as maxInvestment pays for, capped at the freeze quantity.
  • Exit: resting limit sell at entry + priceDiff right after each fill. No stop loss; a leg ends only through its sell or the EOD close.
  • Hedge: every leg is hedged exactly once by buying the opposite option at the strike nearest the money for the same quantity. It fires when the price falls to entry − priceDiff, or when the resting sell fills. The hedge is itself a leg with its own sell and its own one-time hedge, so the chain is unlimited.
  • Direction after a sell: a profitable sell is followed by the same right. A hedge triggered by an adverse move, or by a sell at a loss, buys the opposite right.
  • Caps: maxInvestment gates only the root, never hedges. maxProfit in rupees per broker; on a trip all legs are closed by limit sells that lock the cap, then the strategy stays idle for the day.
  • Stacking: onePositionPerSide (off by default), or optional maxLegsPerSide with stackRecoveryPoints.
  • Double-up: removed 2026-11-13.
  • First-order rule: a root that hits its target with no hedge ever bought restarts contract selection for a fresh root instead of re-buying.

ScaledHedgedStrategy (differences from SimpleContinuous)

  • A. Root size: always initialQuantity, never sized from maxInvestment.
  • B. Hedge size: initialQuantity + (entry − price now) / priceDiff × initialQuantity, floored to whole lots. A CE down 10 gets a PE of 2× quantity. A hedge opened by a target fill buys initialQuantity.
  • C. Falling leg: a leg that falls by priceDiff makes the opposite option be bought again. No double-up.
  • D. One contract per right: one CE and one PE contract per cycle, kept in state.pair. If that contract's leg is open, nothing is bought and no trigger is re-armed; a new leg opens only after it closes. (Rule dated 2026-11-17; before it the ladder added quantity to the open contract.)
  • F. maxInvestment: applies to every buy; a buy is cut to the whole lots that fit, or held back and retried when none fits. Simple applies it to the root only.
  • G. Same-direction re-buy: skipped when that contract already has an open leg.
  • onePositionPerSide: always behaves as on; the config key is ignored.
  • Extra state: an unconfirmed hedge or add buy is persisted as pendingHedge and settled against the broker. Simple resolves unconfirmed buys too, but with no persisted record.

Shared by Simple and Scaled

  • Same PCR root and the same resting-sell-at-priceDiff target.
  • No stop loss.
  • Same rupee maxProfit latch.
  • Per-day JSON state file.
  • Own leg logic rather than LegManager.
