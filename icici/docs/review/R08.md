## Findings

### 1. [MEDIUM] A broker dropped by an AutoStart re-arm forgets its loss/profit lockout across a restart, and can be re-armed the same day
- AutoStart narrows the target's `brokers` to the armable list and writes it to config (AutoStart :245-261). BulkPcr then derives everything durable from `configuredBrokers()`:
  - `persistState` builds `stoppedSlots`, `stoppedBrokers` and `profitStoppedBrokers` from `sideKeys()` / `configuredBrokers()` (BulkPcrStrategy.ts:520-522, 572-574).
  - `loadStopLatches` only loads latches (PnlStop.load and the state-doc copies) inside `for (const broker of this.configuredBrokers())` (:804-830).
  - `pushCap` of the restored cut is also only sent for configured brokers (:806).
- A fresh, never-loaded PnlStop reports `isTripped() == false` (PnlStop.ts:57-59).
- Scenario: AutoStart brokers [kite, ant]. ant's maxLossPerOrder trips at 11:05 with `lossCooldownSeconds: 0` (locked for the day). The 11:10 slot arms with brokers [kite] only; its `persistState` rewrites the state doc without ant's stopped slots. The process restarts. At 11:15 `loadStopLatches` (called by AutoStart before arming) skips ant because the config no longer lists it. `isBrokerStopped('ant')` is false, so ant is in `armable`, and the strategy trades ant again after its loss lock-out. The cut cap is also not re-pushed to the order process for ant until a later `loadStopLatches` runs with ant configured.
- In a live process without restart the in-memory PnlStop objects keep the lock, so the hole is restart-only. Current config uses brokers [kite] only, so it is latent today.
- Direction of fix: persist and load latches for every broker in `PAIR_BROKERS` (or the AutoStart's wanted list), not just the currently configured ones.
- Also answers R05 line 85: the cut is NOT memory-only, it is persisted (`cutsByBroker`, :533) and re-sent on restart (:806), subject to this hole.

### 2. [MEDIUM] Re-buy fills notified while the slot is still `buying` are dropped, leaving filled lots uncounted and unprotected
- `placeRestingRebuy` sets `phase='buying'` (:1731), then places each chunk with a 1 s sleep between chunks (:1740) and only sets `phase='resting'` and `pos.rebuy` after the loop (:1772-1773). `updateTrade` routes a Buy fill to `onRebuyFill` only when `phase==='resting'` (:2331); every other non-`selling` phase returns at :2335.
- Fills and ticks run on different queues (per-strategy FIFO for fills, latest-wins for option ticks, strategiesProcess.ts:215-260), and `buyAndExit` -> `placeRestingRebuy` is fire-and-forget from `onSlotSoldOut` (:2164), so a fill can land inside the placement window.
- Most exposed: the drift re-place (:1920-1925) rests a buy at `roundToTick(ltp)`, close to marketable, with chunks 1 s apart when the quantity exceeds the freeze size.
- Failure scenario: chunk 1 fills during the 1 s sleep and is ignored. `rebuy.filledQty` stays below `rebuy.quantity` forever, so `completeRebuy` never runs; lots are held with no +N sell and no loss stop (`slotUnrealized` only counts `selling` slots, :870). The only recovery is `settleRestingRebuy`, which reads live quantity at :1871 and exits it, and that only runs when the sibling closes, a stop trips or EOD arrives.
- Direction of fix: count Buy fills for a `buying` slot that has `rebuyLimit` (or hold the notifications until `rebuy` is recorded).

### 3. [MEDIUM] `recoverFlatErrors` can clear a slot whose unconfirmed buy order is still live (it checks live qty and pending sells, not pending buys)
- `placeRestingRebuy` sets `phase='error'` with the message "an order may be LIVE" (BulkPcrStrategy.ts:1760-1761) and `buyAndExit` does the same for an unconfirmed market re-buy (:1693-1694). Neither calls `latchLiveRemainder`, so no latch protects the broker.
- Such a slot keeps `heldTsym` from the previous cycle. At the next AutoStart slot, `recoverFlatErrors` (:2942-2968) sees `getLiveQuantity == 0` and no pending SELLS, sets the slot `done` with `abandoned`, and the cycle finishes and re-arms.
- Failure scenario: a re-buy limit placement times out but the order is live at the broker. It fills later with no slot tracking it and no exit; the AutoStart has already re-armed.
- Direction of fix: also require no pending buys for the contract, or latch the live remainder when the placement outcome is unconfirmed.

### 4. [LOW-MEDIUM] AutoStart does not check that the target is reconciled before it resets and arms it
- `attemptStart` calls `target.isRunning()` (phase only) then `target.reset({keepStops:true})` (AutoStart :205, :233) without checking `target.stateLoaded`. `reconcileStrategies` marks each strategy loaded one by one and leaves a failed one unloaded (strategiesProcess.ts:164-173); AutoStart blocks sit after BulkPcr in config.yml, so an AutoStart can be loaded and ticking while its target failed reconcile.
- Effect: `reset` clears `positions` in memory and `persistState()` overwrites the Mongo state doc with no positions (BulkPcrStrategy.ts:2984-2986). A later successful reconcile then has no persisted slots (hedge flag, entryQuantity, rebuy bookkeeping lost) and falls back to the open-trades path, which restores a plain `buying` slot and a deferred exit (:2775-2876).
- Direction of fix: skip the slot if `!target.stateLoaded`.

### 5. [LOW-MEDIUM, order-process side not verified] A late fill notification after a drift cancel could be counted twice
- During `cancelRestingRebuy` fills are ignored via `cancellingRebuy` (:1501, :1787), but only for the duration of the cancel call. `held` is `max(live, notified, cancel.filledQty)` (:1876) and is carried into the new `rebuy.filledQty` (:1772). A polled fill notification for a pre-cancel chunk that is delivered after the new limit is `resting` is added again by `onRebuyFill` (:1788), so `filledQty` can reach `quantity` while fewer lots are held; `completeRebuy` then rests an exit for more lots than held (oversell margin reject or naked short). Whether the order process can deliver such a late notification was not checked.

### 6. [LOW] No `finishIfFlat` after the early close inside `placeExitSellAtTargetPrice`
- When a stop or EOD latch is active the placement squares the fill off and returns (:1275-1281). `buyAndExit` and `completeRebuy` call `finishIfFlat` afterwards, but the callers `enterOnBroker` (:1156), `topUpSlot` (:2051) and `runDeferredRestoreActions` (:1007-1008) do not. If that was the last open slot, the cycle never calls `durablyDisable`: phase stays `running`, config stays `enabled: true`, and every later AutoStart slot logs "already running - skipping". Rare (needs a stop/EOD during the root buy, a top-up or a deferred exit).

### 7. [LOW-MEDIUM] Margin-shrunk top-up never runs for the NIFTY block because its hedges are trailing hedges
- `maybeTopUpHedges` zeroes `topUpQty` and only logs an ALERT for any trail slot (:1998-2003). BulkPcr-NIFTY sets `hedgeTrailDistance: 2` (config.yml block at :203-226), and margin shrink applies to hedge buys (they bypass the cap, :1666), so the feature.md top-up claim does not apply to the NIFTY configuration; a shrunk hedge stays under-sized for the cycle. Behaviour is deliberate in code but contradicts the feature description.

### 8. [LOW] Optional numeric keys are read raw and become NaN when absent
- `onLossTrip` does `cutsByBroker + cfg().lossInvestmentCut` and `cfg().lossCooldownSeconds * 1000` (:637-638); `hedgeAtPercentOfStop` and `lossInvestmentCut` have no validator entry (configValidation.ts:23-24 covers only the cooldown/trail/slDistance keys, and only when present; :329 requires only quantity, targetPoints, slDistance).
- A block missing `lossInvestmentCut` makes the cut NaN: `effectiveCap` NaN, `pushCap` sends NaN to the order process (:620), the profit stop's cap base is NaN, and `maybeReleaseLoss` gives up ("cap is NaN"). Both current blocks set it, so latent.

### 9. [LOW] feature.md section 6 understates the hedge behaviour
- Code opens the hedge also on `hedgeAtPercentOfStop` (default 50, NIFTY block 70) of the per-order rupee stop (:1590-1596), and runs the pair on kite and breeze as well as ANT. feature.md line 102 says "ANT opposite-side pair hedge on `slDistance`". Also the comment at :612 says loss and profit percentages are both taken of the cut cap, but code takes the loss stop of the base cap (:599-600) and the profit stop of the cut cap (:751), so maxProfit shrinks with each loss cut (not documented).

### 10. [LOW] Crash between cancel and re-place in `topUpSlot` leaves a `selling` slot with no resting sell, trusted by reconcile
- `topUpSlot` cancels the resting sell (:2029), buys (:2048) and re-places (:2051); the slot stays persisted as `selling`. The same-day reconcile branch (:2632-2664) only confirms the position is open, and only checks resting sells for a trail slot with a recorded exit. A crash in that window restores a slot that believes an exit is resting. (If `topUpQty` was still persisted > 0 the next tick re-runs the top-up and re-places the sell.)

### 11. [LOW] Smaller points
- `lastStuckCause` in AutoStart is never cleared on a new day (AutoStart :61, :75-81), so the same errored-broker cause is not re-alerted tomorrow.
- `enterOnBroker`'s one retry (:1207-1208) does not re-check `isBrokerStopped` for the 10 s+ gap; only `placeExitSellAtTargetPrice` squares it off afterwards (:1275).
- `placingKeys` is a Set, not a counter: an `onOrderCancelled` re-placement running concurrently with an in-flight placement removes the key when the first finishes (:1322), re-enabling `closeBroker` while the second is still placing (:2196).
- `exitRejectPolicy` use is correct: counts per position, `marginShortfall` -> `error`, `tooMany` -> `error` (:2430-2445); the documented per-chunk limitation (:178-188) remains.

## Checked and found sound (no finding)
- Double-order guards: `buying` is set synchronously before any await in `openOppositeSide` (:1609), `buyAndExit` (:1655), `placeRestingRebuy` (:1731) and `completeRebuy` (:1799); the hedge opens only when exactly one non-done slot exists (:1533) and not after `abandoned` (:1526).
- Sign/size math: hedge quantity = root still-open quantity (:1604); `slotUnrealized` = (ltp - entry) x open (:873); `dayPnl` = min(realized, realized+unrealized) (:886-889); NaN P&L is not booked (:846) and non-finite ticks are rejected before the hedge trigger (:1510).
- Per-broker keying: stops by slot key, profit stop/cooldowns/cuts/day P&L by broker, ltp by slot key; legacy bare-name state is migrated (:2516-2527).
- Restart: a reconcile places no order; exit re-placement, lock-out close and re-buy settle are deferred to `runDeferredRestoreActions` and re-read resting sells before placing (:976-981). `lastLtpByKey` is deleted on sold-out and EOD (:2148, :2241), so a rolled re-buy contract has no stale tick.
- `enabled:false`: ticks, fills and cancellations are not delivered to a disabled strategy (dispatcher gate), and reconcile restores without placing orders.
