## 2. Definitions (replace "Forecast" family; keep Platform, Trading Day)

- **"Platform"**: the Company's web application at nivedhatech.com.
- **"Recommendation"**: a research recommendation submitted by the Contractor on the Platform for a NIFTY index option, stating the contract (strike, CE or PE), an entry price, a target price and a stop-loss price.
- **"Approved Recommendation"**: a Recommendation the Company has approved on the Platform. A Recommendation the Company rejects, or does not act on by the end of the day, is not an Approved Recommendation.
- **"Resulting Trade"**: the trade, if any, that the Company places in its own account because of an Approved Recommendation.
- **"Trade Profit"**: for each Resulting Trade, the exit value minus the entry value of that trade for the contract concerned, before taxes, but after the Company's brokerage and statutory charges on that trade, as recorded in the Company's trade records. A loss is a Trade Loss.
- **"Profit Share"**: the percentage in Schedule 1 of the net Trade Profit for a settlement week, as defined in clause 6A.2.
- **"Settlement Week"**: Wednesday to the following Tuesday (both days included).

## 3. Nature of the Engagement (replace clauses 3.1 to 3.4)

1. The Contractor is engaged as an **independent contractor** to provide research recommendations. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, agency or investment-management relationship.
2. The Contractor is not a client of the Company, invests no money, holds no interest in the Company's account or capital, and does not share in any loss. The Contractor's only entitlement is the Fee under Section 6A.
3. The Contractor has no authority to place, direct, modify, approve or cancel any order, no access to any broker or trading account of the Company, and no authority to bind the Company.
4. The Contractor shall provide the services through one designated individual, **[name]** (the "Designated Forecaster"), who must hold a separate personal invitation to the Platform. The Contractor may not change that person without the Company's written consent and is responsible for that person's compliance with this Agreement.

## 4. The Services (replace)

1. The Contractor will submit Recommendations on the Platform. Each Recommendation must be the Contractor's own research, prepared in compliance with the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and any circulars issued under them, and with the disclosures, records and rationale those rules require.
2. A Recommendation is valid only if the stop-loss is below the entry price and the entry price is below the target, and it is made for a contract not already traded that day. The Contractor may have one open Recommendation at a time.
3. The Contractor must not copy, resell or relay calls from third parties.
4. A Recommendation cannot be edited or withdrawn once recorded, but it lapses at the end of the day if not approved.
5. The Contractor must not access the Platform through bots or scripts without the Company's written consent.

## 5. How the Company Uses Recommendations (replace)

1. **The Company decides.** The Company reviews every Recommendation and may approve or reject it, with or without giving reasons, in its sole discretion. The Company has no obligation to approve, to trade on, or to continue trading on any Recommendation.
2. **The Company controls execution.** Only the Company places orders, in its own account with its own capital and its own broker. The Contractor never places, modifies or cancels an order, and has no access to the Company's broker or account.
3. **Recommendations are one input.** The Company may combine a Recommendation with its own analysis and risk limits, and may exit a Resulting Trade at any time.

## 5A. What the Contractor Can See (new)

1. The Platform shows the Contractor, **read-only**: its own Recommendations and their status; and, for each of its own Resulting Trades, the **entry price and the live profit or loss**.
2. The Contractor cannot see the Company's broker, account balances, capital, order details, or any trade that did not result from the Contractor's own Approved Recommendations, and has no control over any trade.
3. Information visible to the Contractor is confidential under Section 10. Its visibility does not give the Contractor any interest in, or right over, any trade or position.

## 6. Fees: general (replace; keep the title "Fees")

The Contractor is paid solely as described in Section 6A. There is no retainer under this version unless Schedule 1 states one.

## 6A. Fee on Approved Recommendations (new)

**6A.1 Profit Share.** For each Settlement Week the Company will pay the Contractor the Profit Share percentage in Schedule 1 of the **net Trade Profit** of all Resulting Trades of that contractor that were **closed** in that week, where net Trade Profit is the total of the Trade Profit of those trades after setting off Trade Losses of the same week.

**6A.2 No carry-forward, no clawback.** If the net result of a Settlement Week is zero or a loss, no Profit Share is payable for that week. A loss in one week is never carried forward and never reduces any Profit Share for another week or any amount already paid. **No amount is ever payable by the Contractor to the Company, and nothing is deducted from the Contractor for any loss.**

**6A.3 Payment.** The Company will pay the Profit Share by bank transfer on the Wednesday after the Settlement Week, or the next working day, against the invoice required in Section 8, subject to tax under Section 8.

**6A.4 Records.** The Profit Share is computed from the Company's own trade records. The Platform will show the Contractor a statement for each Settlement Week. The Contractor must raise any disagreement within 15 days of the payment, and the parties will review the records together in good faith.

**6A.5 Contractor's representation about its Registration (new).** The Contractor represents and confirms that:
- (a) the Registration is valid, and it will tell the Company within 3 days if it lapses, is suspended or is cancelled;
- (b) it has checked its Registration conditions, the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations and any SEBI circular on research analyst fees, and its own compliance function (or compliance officer, where it has one) has confirmed that **receiving the Profit Share under this Agreement is permitted for the Contractor**; and
- (c) it will tell the Company at once if that position changes.

**6A.6 Change of fee model.** If the Contractor or the Company concludes that the Profit Share is not permitted for the Contractor, or the Company gives 15 days' notice under Section 15, the Fee will instead be the **fixed fee per Approved Recommendation** stated in Schedule 1, which is not linked to any Trade Profit or Trade Loss. The change applies only to Recommendations approved after it takes effect and does not alter any statement already issued.

**6A.7 Company's rights.** The Company may suspend Recommendations at any time. If the Registration is not valid, no Recommendation is accepted, and no Fee accrues for Recommendations made while it was invalid.

## 9. Contractor Undertakings (replace the bullets that refer to forecasts)

During the term of this Agreement the Contractor shall:
1. provide accurate identity, PAN, GSTIN, address, bank and Registration details, and keep them up to date;
2. keep its Registration valid and comply with the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations in preparing Recommendations;
3. not give the same Recommendation to another person for the purpose of the Company's trade, and not execute, or ask anyone to execute, any trade in the Company's account;
4. not share its Platform login, and report any unauthorised use immediately;
5. not use any non-public information about the Company or the Platform for its own trading or for anyone else's;
6. not describe itself as associated with, or endorsed by, the Company's broker.

Each party is responsible for its own compliance with applicable law, including any registration the law may require.

## 13. Term and Termination (add one bullet)

The Company may end this Agreement immediately by email if the Registration lapses, is suspended or cancelled, or if the representation in clause 6A.5 is untrue.

## 14. Liability (replace the second bullet)

The Company's total liability is limited to Profit Share earned and not yet paid. The Contractor is never liable for a Trade Loss of the Company.
