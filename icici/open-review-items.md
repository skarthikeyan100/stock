## Per-trade loss budget as an exit (2026-11-25)

User decision: the loss cap no longer shrinks a manual buy's size (it did: 7 capital lots became 1, `resolveManualBuyQuantity`). It is enforced where the stop is attached: `lossBudgetStopPoints` (order stop = min(configured, budget / quantity, add-more combined)), `tightenStopToLossBudget` (merged position after chunks / add-more), `capRestoredStop` (restart). Fixed 2026-11-25 (live case 12:26 NIFTY26D0125800PE, 1040 qty, GTT 346264900 + 346265018): an add-more over a Kite GTT / ANT bracket arms an in-app hard stop at the budget stop (`trade.hardStopPrice`, honoured by `exitMonitor` even when watch-only), and for Kite `kiteExecutor.reprotectToBudget` deletes every old GTT (confirmed gone in the GTT book; a triggered one is left to the poll), then places ONE GTT for the merged quantity at the budget stop and the target (retries 1/3/8 s, ALERT, in-app exit if it fails; an uncancellable old GTT is kept and nothing new is placed); the GTT poll re-checks every 60 s (`checkGttsAgainstBudget`, covers restart). Remaining: an ANT bracket cannot be re-priced (in-app hard stop only); a restart between the cancel and the new placement leaves the in-app monitor as the exit until restart restore; a merged quantity above one freeze order has no GTT (in-app exit); prism has no manual-buy loss-budget path. Open: (1) see above; (2) partial fills keep the stop computed for the intended quantity (tighter than needed); (3) the stop is derived from the sizing quote, the fill price can differ by ticks (the merged-position tighten corrects the aggregate); (4) a user with no target configured gets no exit armed (finalizeEntry needs target and stop); (5) lotCount users have no budget (as before).
